Strategic Investment Platform
Founded · 2026 · Saudi Arabia

Minority Partners is a Saudi Arabia-headquartered strategic investment platform founded in 2026, operating across three disciplines: Value Creation Investments for strategic minority equity positions, Urban & Infrastructure Partnerships for public-private development initiatives, and Bridge Investments for cross-border emerging market connectivity.

Mission

Patient capital, enduring outcomes.

Minority Partners deploys strategic capital where governance, alignment, and patient conviction compound into enduring outcomes. The firm partners selectively — choosing relationships where long-term value creation is a shared objective, not a constraint.

The firm's mandate covers minority investment positions across three disciplines: value-chain minority equity, public-private infrastructure partnerships, and cross-market bridge investments. In each case, the ambition is the same — to deploy capital that remains relevant and productive across decades, not cycles.

Geography

Where we operate.

Headquartered in Madinah, Saudi Arabia, Minority Partners operates within the Saudi Vision 2030 context while maintaining an internationally oriented investment mandate. The firm's geographic reach extends through an offshore entity in Abu Dhabi — providing access to GCC capital markets — and a market development presence in the Horn of Africa, where emerging-market bridge opportunities are actively cultivated.

Philosophy

The pace of conviction.

Minority Partners does not move at the pace of market cycles. Investment decisions are driven by conviction — in the quality of the partner, the durability of the opportunity, and the strength of the alignment. The firm measures its work across decades, not quarters.

This philosophy shapes every aspect of the firm's approach: the length of its investment horizons (10 to 25 years), its preference for minority positions that allow partners to retain operational authority, and its focus on governance structures that create durable value regardless of market conditions.

The outcome is a portfolio of relationships and positions built to outlast capital cycles — institutions strengthened, infrastructure matured, and partnerships that continue to generate value long after initial deployment.
